**Frozen:** 2026-10-31 JST. **Status:** Scientific definitions FIXED before any prediction–reference outcome calculation in this workflow; execution is subject to the explicit quality gates below. **Authoritative document:** this file supersedes the 2026-10-30 freeze *candidate* and the separate 2026-10-31 helix/H-bond annex where incompatible. ## 0. Research question, study units and hypothesis boundary

> For one fixed publicly released Boltz-2 human MPC1L–MPC2 complex prediction, how much does its agreement with experimental complex coordinates vary as a function of four chosen experimental reference structures when the same experimentally defined common-interface residue mask is held constant, and can differences be described consistently by whole-interface geometry and predeclared local state-related residue geometry?

**Single model, four structural references, not four independent replicates.** Do not infer general accuracy, causal effects of inhibitors, thermodynamic binding free energies, physiological transport cycle, or model training/generalization ability. Cross-sectional 2025 human experimental structures share related purification/refinement and possible structure-template bias. One inward-open apo structure vs three inhibitor-bound outward-open structures **confounds conformation with ligand treatment**; do not attribute reference-dependent deviations solely to conformational state. Experimental auxiliary PMb25 macrobody and membrane/detergent conditions are limitations. Relevant paper: Sichrovsky et al., *Science Advances* (2025), DOI `10.1126/sciadv.adw1489`, and its supplement. Atlas training structure cutoff reportedly 2023-06-01 and four PDBs released 2025-05-07; absence of these PDBs from direct training is not proof of absence of indirect information leakage; exact atlas checkpoint hash unverified.

## 3. Comparisons and primary numerical definitions

Exactly **four** prediction-vs-native: (Boltz,9GIX), (Boltz,9GIV), (Boltz,9GIW), (Boltz,9GIY). Exactly **six** unordered native-vs-native controls: GIX–GIV, GIX–GIW, GIX–GIY, GIV–GIW, GIV–GIY, GIW–GIY. Fixed lexicographic reference ordering for reproducing transformations; RMSD itself is symmetric under optimal same-set fit. No significance tests or pseudo-replication.

**Primary endpoint:** `fixed_union5_interface_CA_RMSD_A`: 73 matched Cα coordinates across both chains; calculate proper Kabsch rotation+translation with det(R)=+1 minimizing mean squared distance; compute RMSD on the **same 73 points** after the fit. No scaling, no mirror reflection, no per-chain independent fit. Save residues in deterministic MPC1L then MPC2 ascending order, 73 identities, fitting transform matrix/determinant, squared residuals and input hashes. This is **not** official DockQ iRMSD.

**Required sensitivity/control metrics** on the same 10 comparison pairs: fixed-62 matched Cα two-chain fit RMSD (`fixed_union4_interface_CA_RMSD_A`), full fixed-198 matched Cα two-chain fit RMSD; independent MPC1L-only 84-Cα RMSD and MPC2-only 114-Cα RMSD; fit MPC2 114, evaluate transformed MPC1L 84 *without refitting* and conversely fit MPC1L 84 and evaluate MPC2 114. Different fits answer different questions; each must carry fitted/evaluated mask identifiers. For native/native controls do not compress inward/outward and outward/outward into statistical samples. Do not privilege a reference after seeing results. For uncertainty, report raw contrasts and context rather than inferential confidence intervals.

**Numerical preflight:** identical-input RMSD <1e-5 Å, Kabsch rigid-transform and atom-order invariance within tolerance, proper rotation, chain swap/mismatched sequence negative controls, exact primary/sensitivity/global counts and CA coordinate presence. Failing a mandatory test => STOP Primary before reporting any result. No posthoc threshold/mask change.

## 5. H1 geometry: replace failed short-window PCA with fixed local-axis method

**Old method permanently REJECTED for H1:** PCA axes on MPC1L 26–30 vs 38–45 and MPC2 40–47 vs 57–61 failed ±1 residue robustness, with worst changes 16.5–70° across five structures. **Selected pre-outcome H1 method:** HELANAL-type local helical axes from four successive Cα positions, consistent with the installed `MDAnalysis.analysis.helix_analysis.helix_analysis` implementation; method/provenance and code source hash archived.

Frozen H1 analysis windows: **MPC1L residues 26–48**, nominated hinge **33**; **MPC2 residues 40–61**, hinge **53**. For window [a,b], use consecutive 4-CA windows starting at every integer s=a,...,b-3; calculate HELANAL local unit axis for each. Orient every axis to positive dot with the *whole-window* endpoint displacement Cα(b)−Cα(a) (if dot ≈0, metric NOT QUALIFIED). Define left local-axis starts **s≤hinge−4** and right starts **s≥hinge+1**; do not include the hinge-adjacent intervening local axes in the two mean vectors. Average oriented left axes, normalize, likewise right; directed bend angle `acos(clamp(u_left·u_right,-1,1))` degrees (0–180). Record n_left/n_right, all local axes, bend profile and mean resultant length (near-zero resultant => NOT QUALIFIED). Require at least two axes each side. This is a **descriptive curvature proxy**, not exact physical hinge angle or original paper's bundle rotation.

**Stability flag frozen:** evaluate each nonempty combination of full, first-axis-dropped and last-axis-dropped lists on each side, requiring at least two axes. Maximum absolute angle difference against full estimate; flag **`H1_NUMERIC_SENSITIVITY_GT5`** if **strictly >5.0°**. This is a computational sensitivity threshold and not a hypothesis test. Pre-freeze measured across five inputs: MPC1L variation 2.714–4.144°, MPC2 2.413–5.019°. Specifically **9GIX MPC2 5.019° is flagged**; do not round or change cutoff, or label all PASS. Every H1 angle, including flagged, may be tabulated with uncertainty flag, but flagged estimates cannot support robust quantitative hinge magnitude comparisons. Dependence on alternate hinge-center and windows **has not been comprehensively tested**; do not claim global robustness. Note this sensitivity perturbation is not equivalent to old PCA endpoint ±1 QC. Frozen windows must not be optimized for model-native agreement; if no local axes or coverage, mark that metric `NOT EVALUABLE`.

## 6. H2/H3 bundle geometry and non-identifiable reorientation

Operational H2/H3 Cα segments: MPC1L H2 54–75, H3 79–105; MPC2 H2 68–89, H3 93–121. Within each structure, compute first PCA axes using centered Cα positions of these longer segments, orient N→C, report directed and acute (`acos(abs(dot))`) H2-versus-H3 angle; store eigenvalue anisotropy and segment/atom coverage. **This within-state H2/H3 angle is NOT the published 16.5°/19° between-state rigid bundle rotation.**

**Between-state H2/H3 rigid rotation is an OPTIONAL QUALIFICATION-GATED secondary endpoint, not a minimum success criterion.** To make it physically interpretable a state-independent stable reference frame inside each subunit must first be supported by non-outcome-driven geometry and structural annotation. No such H1 core has yet passed a pre-outcome robustness QC; therefore the default for the frozen minimum is `NOT QUALIFIED / NOT RUN` for any numeric between-state bundle rotation. **Do not** choose a novel fit segment based on observed prediction/native differences, do not fit H2/H3 to itself and call its fit rotation the biological domain movement, and do not claim a reproduction of the original paper's 16.5°/19°. Preserve qualitative structure overlay images if useful with explicit fit selection; no numeric bundle-rotation claim unless a separately versioned, pre-outcome amendment qualifies and pins a stable reference, with new Gate recorded. This protects the valid H1, H2/H3 and primary analyses from an ill-defined coordinate-frame quantity.

This counts solvent occlusion with a conventional per-side representation, not direct binding energy. Artificial-chain QC and translation invariance PASS, full C01-structure atom classification remains an execution gate.

## 8. Interchain salt-bridge and hydrogen-bond screens (exploratory, all five structures)

**Salt bridges, Tier 0 unmodified coordinates:** geometric candidate when any interchain atom from **Lys NZ or Arg NE/NH1/NH2** to **Asp OD1/OD2 or Glu OE1/OE2** has distance **strictly <4.0 Å**. Log nearest atom pair, minimum Å and a unique residue pair; exclude His in this fixed criterion, disallow use of modeled OXT as original-structure bridge, and mark absent functional atom `NOT EVALUABLE` (not 0). Count not thermodynamic strength; watch local cryoEM support.

**Hydrogen bonds Tier 1 (original heavy atoms):** enumerate chemically typed plausible interchain **donor heavy atom–acceptor heavy atom** pairs with **D–A≤3.0 Å**, residue-level and atom-level IDs. Fixed typing: backbone N donor except Pro; backbone carbonyl O acceptor; Asn/Gln side-chain amide N donor, carbonyl O acceptor; Lys/Arg N donor; Asp/Glu carboxyl O acceptor; Ser/Thr/Tyr hydroxyl O both potential donor and acceptor; Trp indole NE1 donor; His excluded from charged/tautomer-dependent primary tally unless explicitly represented as variant (Tier 2 only); Cys thiol and ambiguous terminal groups excluded from primary typing unless predeclared in source atom typing table. Neither backbone N of Pro nor protonated carboxylate donor is assumed. Treat peptide amide N as not acceptor. Nonstandard residues untyped => NOT EVALUABLE. This is **potential D/A proximity, NOT a confirmed hydrogen bond**. Record unclassified atom sites. A pair with both chemically possible roles may yield two oriented candidates; count unique residue pairs separately from oriented atom pairs.

**Hydrogen bonds Tier 2 (versioned protonation-model-dependent):** OpenMM **8.2** + PDBFixer **1.12.0**, `amber14-all.xml`, pH **7.0** as computational assumption, His **HIE** throughout (document that this is not proven biological microstate), force field default Asp/Glu anionic and Lys/Arg cationic, chain termini treated by force field. Add *only* force-field-required **OXT** at detected chain endpoints using PDBFixer after `findMissingResidues`, `findNonstandardResidues`, `findMissingAtoms`; set `missingResidues={}` explicitly, reject unexpected internal heavy atom completion or nonstandard residue replacement. **Do not** fit, minimize, or rebuild native observed protein heavy atoms. Make distinct hydrogenated DERIVED copy with provenance; never feed its modeled OXT/H back to original contact masks, FreeSASA or primary RMSD. OpenMM `Modeller.addHydrogens` generates bonded H; after addition verify original full heavy-atom IDs and max change ≤`1e-5 Å`, valid forcefield system, positive H count, His variants and residue charge states (or force-field charge accounting), and save coordinates/topology/input hashes. For each donor D bonded H and interchain acceptor A, screen **D–A ≤3.0 Å** and **D–H–A ≥150°**, count unique D–H–A triplets and residue pairs separately; exclude artificial-terminus contacts and His-involving pairs from the *core Tier 2 count* unless explicitly isolated and labeled as protonation-sensitive; tabulate separately if desired. No distance/angle adjustment after seeing results. The angle is at H (D–H–A); do not confuse it with D–A–H.

**Tier 2 prerequisite results:** naive RDKit protein `AddHs` **NOT QUALIFIED** (neutral Lys/Asp/Glu charge artifacts); on actual 9GIV two-chain PDB initial OpenMM failed because of missing C-terminal OXT at A GLY106 / B ALA125; targeted PDBFixer+OpenMM retry **PASS**: original 1628 heavy; +2 OXT =1630; +1651 H =3281 particles; original heavy displacement 0.0 Å after repair, ≤7.950e−6 Å after H placement; force field assigned successfully. This qualifies **basic hydrogen placement on 9GIV only**, not biological protonation truth nor HB detection algorithm or all five C01 protein inputs. Require same per-structure QC for other four; if fails mark **only that structure's Tier 2 `NOT RUN`**. A deterministic synthetic positive/negative D–H–A angle/distance unit test is required at execution **before reporting Tier 2 counts**. If that test cannot be passed, mark Tier 2 globally `NOT RUN`; Tier 1 and geometric salt continue. Any changes to protonation assumptions after seeing structures' outcome counts require an openly versioned exploratory analysis, never a stealth edit to this protocol.

## 9. Experimental quality context and interpretation limits

Four downloaded wwPDB validation XML/PDF reports were locally audited; do not call validation complete for every hydrogen bond. Native experimental map resolutions ~9GIX 3.65, 9GIV 3.65, 9GIW 3.92, 9GIY 3.79 Å; residue-level Qscore and map inclusion vary substantially, notably MPC1L E51, MPC2 K66 and 9GIW MPC2 I90. For each local sidechain contact include its corresponding available residue-level map quality score, atom inclusion, B-factor with definition, and `UNAVAILABLE` where absent. Macrobody and shared starting refinement model (9GIV template used for others) reduce independence. Sidechain proximity is an atomic model hypothesis, not hydrogen atom experimentally resolved; no energy/affinity/mechanism inference. Single-PPI novelty needs independent downstream evaluation versus original Sichrovsky paper/SI and Atlas analyses.

Afterwards assess whether there is a **nonredundant scientifically useful contribution** beyond already known state structure and public prediction benchmarking; distinguish Scientific Validity, MSC, preprint suitability and public data value. The research's publish decision is not decided by protocol QC.

**STOP rule:** 30-day initial research cycle, typically 30–60-minute sessions. Minimum endpoint is the 4+6 fixed-73 comparisons, prespecified controls, QC and interpretable limitations, NOT infinite exploratory refinement. Primary gate failure => STOP and report. Failure of a particular exploratory software/tool => mark relevant metric `NOT RUN` and move on without claiming zero. A revision after viewing any prediction/native RMSD requires a dated amendment with full explanation, explicit separation of prespecified vs posthoc results; original `v1.0 FINAL` retained immutable.
